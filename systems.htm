STORE System

A cognitive recovery protocol designed to escape "dopamine holes" and restore baseline productivity.

S – Stop Digging

Immediately sever cheap dopamine loops. Close tabs, isolate the phone, and remove external stimuli to allow the brain to reset.

T – Tune into Body

Trigger a physical state-change. Cold exposure, 30-second breathing holds, or high-intensity bursts (15 pushups) to generate raw energy.

O – One Small Win

Leverage the energy surge for a singular, easy task (e.g., washing one dish, making the bed) to prove the capacity for movement.

R – Regulate (15m)

Engage in a low-dopamine stabilizing habit. Read 3 pages, do a technology-free walk, or cook a nutritious meal.

Feedback Loop: Identify the trigger (fatigue, boredom, avoidance) and adjust the environmental boundaries to prevent re-entry.
