Keystone State Challenge Academy

Residential Military-Style Training | Jan 2024 – Spring 2024

Located at Fort Indiantown Gap, the KSCA is a National Guard Youth ChalleNGe program designed to foster structure, discipline, and essential adult life skills through a rigorous military training model.

Mission

To provide a path toward self-discipline, education, and the skills necessary to succeed as productive, responsible citizens through a safe and structured residential experience.

Vision

To be a life-changing opportunity for every Cadet, providing a fresh start to find renewed success, improve academic standing, and increase potential for future employment.

The 8 Core Components

Mastery of these eight pillars is required for program completion, providing a solid foundation for continued success.

Academic Excellence

Focusing on GED preparation and credit recovery through intensive daily classes certified by the CAIU, ensuring a return to academic strength.

Physical Training

Daily fitness activities aimed at meeting high standards in timed one-mile runs, sit-ups, and push-ups to build endurance and strength.

Service to the Community

Minimum of 40 hours of volunteer work for community service and conservation projects, fostering a spirit of selfless service.

Leadership & Followership

Hands-on development of teamwork and command roles, teaching when to lead and how to be an effective part of a larger unit.

Responsible Citizenship

Instruction on the US Government structure, individual rights, and the responsibilities of being a positive member of society.

Life Coping Skills

Training designed to increase self-awareness, self-discipline, and independent living skills necessary for adult independence.

Health & Hygiene

Comprehensive classes on nutrition, disease prevention, substance abuse awareness, and maintaining personal physical readiness.

Job Skills

Direct exploration and preparation for career options, building the professional toolkit needed for the modern workforce.

The KSCA Curriculum

Program Duration 22-Week Residential

A high-intensity, structured environment focused on transforming behavioral outcomes and educational trajectories through constant oversight and feedback.

Facility Fort Indiantown Gap (Annville, PA)

Housed within a military installation, providing an immersive atmosphere of accountability, order, and professionalism.
